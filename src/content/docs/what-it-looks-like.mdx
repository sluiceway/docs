## The parts

Read the dashboard from the top. Two sections this example does not show come before Pending.
While a stack deploys, its section comes before every other and each of its rows starts with a
<a href={glossary("Spinner")}>spinner</a>, a small moving crate. With merge and deploy on,
Updates waiting to merge lists each <a href={glossary("Update waiting to merge")}>pull request one tick merges and deploys</a>,
and under them a line with no box, ending in `waits on its checks`, for each
<a href={glossary("Update waiting on its checks")}>update waiting on its checks</a>. That line
gets its box once its checks are green.

- **The <a href={glossary("Header")}>header</a>.** Penny with four crates upstream, one per pending stack, and the delete
  sign, because a pending row deletes resources. A row that replaces a resource would add the
  replace sign. <a href={ROUTES.theHeader}>The header</a> shows every picture.
- **The <a href={glossary("Counts line")}>counts line</a>.** How many stacks are pending, deploying, preview failed and in
  sync, always all four, each behind its <a href={glossary("Count dot")}>count dot</a>, and how many pending stacks
  destroy resources. Under it, the
  <a href={glossary("Scan line")}>scan line</a> says which commit the last scan checked out, when, in which run, and when the
  last full scan was.
- **The <a href={glossary("Destroy alert")}>destroy alert</a>.** A caution block above the pending rows names every
  pending stack that deletes or replaces resources, here `apps/legacy-worker:prod`, and in a
  paragraph of its own every drifted stack with a resource gone outside the code. The deletes
  themselves stay under that stack's row.
- **A <a href={glossary("Pending")}>pending</a> row.** `apps/api:prod` has one update waiting. Its row links the preview,
  names the pull requests that brought the change, links the compare view, and folds the
  changes under their count. Tick its box to deploy that stack exactly as its row shows it.
- **A row with deletes.** `apps/legacy-worker:prod` deletes three resources. A
  <a href={glossary("Destroy")}>destroy</a> is listed first, outside the fold, and always carries a warning.
- **A row with <a href={glossary("Change outside a stack")}>changes outside the stack</a>.** `apps/web:prod` names the pull request its stack claims and counts two
  changes that no stack claims, such as a lockfile bump, since they may reach any stack.
- **The <a href={glossary("Bulk box")}>bulk box</a>.** `Deploy all 4 pending stacks`, under the pending rows. A tick on it
  deploys nothing. The next run puts a <a href={glossary("Confirm box")}>confirm box</a> in its place,
  <code class="sw-code-long">Confirm: deploy all 4 pending stacks</code>, which names the stacks and who asked. Ticking the
  confirm box is what deploys them, as a tick on each row: every stack is judged by its own
  <a href={glossary("Tick rule")}>tick rule</a> and gets its own fresh preview. If a row changes before anyone ticks it, the
  confirm box goes and the bulk box comes back. Under drifted rows the box reads
  `Repair all N drifted stacks`. A section with one row has no bulk box, and neither has a
  dashboard with deploys off or a read-only one.
- **<a href={glossary("In sync")}>In sync</a>.** The stacks with nothing to deploy, folded under their count.
- **Recently deployed, the <a href={glossary("Trail")}>trail</a>.** Every deploy from the dashboard that ended, newest
  first, one line each: its <a href={glossary("Result dot")}>result dot</a>, the stack id, its <a href={glossary("Ticker")}>ticker</a>,
  the date and time in UTC, and a link to its run. A failed deploy is listed too, with its
  failure reason, and stays listed when a later deploy of its stack clears the <a href={glossary("Failure line")}>failure line</a> on
  the row. A deploy that went out can have a <a href={glossary("Shipped line")}>shipped line</a> under it, and a full scan adds
  the <a href={glossary("Outside deploy")}>outside deploys</a> it finds in the tool's own history, which this example does not show.
- **The <a href={glossary("Rescan box")}>rescan box</a>.** Tick it to start a full scan of every stack. It deploys nothing.
