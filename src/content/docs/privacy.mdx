## What is counted

For each page view, the Umami script sends the page's address without its query string, the
page title, the address of the page you came from, your screen size and your browser's language.
The Umami server works out your browser, operating system and device type from your browser's
user agent, and your country, region and city from your IP address. It does not store the IP
address.

It also counts these events:

- **A search.** The words you typed into the search box, up to 100 characters, and how many
  results it found. It is sent once you stop typing for a moment or open a result.
- **Copied code.** The page, and the title of the code block you copied, or its language.
- **Page feedback.** The page, and whether you answered yes or no to "Was this page useful?".
- **A click on a link to another site.** The site's address without the page. For a link to
  Sluiceway's repository on GitHub or to its Marketplace page, the full address.

{WAITLIST_URL && <p>A click on the footer's link to the hosted version is counted too, as a click on that link and nothing else.</p>}

Umami counts only at the address these docs are published at, so a copy of them running anywhere
else sends nothing.

## Where it goes

Umami runs on our own machine, at analytics.robbeverhelst.be. What it counts stays there. It is
not shared with anyone, and nothing leaves that machine.

## How long it is kept

Everything is kept until we delete it. Nothing deletes it automatically.

## What is stored in your browser

Counting sets no cookie and stores nothing in your browser.

The docs themselves keep two settings in your browser, and send neither anywhere: the colour
theme you pick, in local storage, and which sidebar groups are open, in session storage, which
your browser clears when you close the tab.

## Do Not Track

If your browser sends Do Not Track, the Umami script is not loaded and nothing is counted.

## How to object

Email [robbe.verhelst@gmail.com](mailto:robbe.verhelst@gmail.com) to object, or to ask for what
was counted about your visits to be deleted.
