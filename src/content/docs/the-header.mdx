Upstream is on the left, where crates pile up while changes are pending. Downstream is on the
right, where the water runs while a stack deploys. Each picture has a light and a dark file,
and the dashboard shows the one that matches your GitHub theme.

## With the destroy sign

When a pending or deploying row deletes or replaces a resource, the pending and deploying
pictures carry the destroy sign: an amber warning triangle on a pole in the water. It does not
move, and nothing else in the picture changes.

## Water steps

The water upstream of Penny rises in five steps, with one amber mark each on the gauge.

## Without the header

With <a href={route("configuration/#dashboardpersonality")}><code>dashboard.personality: false</code></a>{" "}
the dashboard shows no header and none of the two voiced lines, and keeps the counts, the rows
and the plain wording.
