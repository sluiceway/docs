## Signs for deletes and replaces

The pending, queued, deploying and failing pictures carry a sign when a pending, queued or
deploying row has a delete or a replace.

- **A replace** adds the replace sign, an amber warning triangle on a pole in the water.
- **A delete** adds the delete sign, an amber diamond with a cross, under the replace sign.

The signs do not move, and nothing else in the picture changes.

## The water rises in five steps

Behind the closed or jammed gate the water rises in five steps, with one amber mark each on the
gauge. Behind the open gate of a deploy it stays at one level.

## The spinner is not a header

A deploying or queued row starts with the <a href={glossary("Spinner")}>spinner</a>, a small
crate on its way through the gate. It comes from the same release tag as the header, and it
says only that the row's deploy is on its way, not the state of the dashboard, so it is not in
the pictures above.

## Without the header

With <a href={ROUTES.personality}><code>dashboard.personality: false</code></a>{" "}
the dashboard shows no header, no spinner and none of the voiced lines, and keeps the
counts, the rows and the plain wording.
