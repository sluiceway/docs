<p class="sw-lead">
  A plan decides how many adopted repos the app shows whole, how far back the audit log goes,
  and whether a second approver can hold a deploy. Every other feature is on every plan.

| Plan | Adopted repos | History |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | 5 | 30 days |
| Team | 25 | 13 months |
| Team Plus | 100 | 3 years |

A required second approver is on Team and Team Plus: you add Sluiceway as a custom deployment
protection rule on a GitHub environment, and GitHub holds each deploy job there until someone
other than the ticker approves it in the app. GitHub offers custom protection rules on GitHub
Enterprise and on public repos only. On Free the app approves such a job at once, so no deploy
waits.

The prices are on the <a href="https://sluiceway.dev/pricing">pricing page</a>.

A plan belongs to an installation: one org, or one personal account, on GitHub.

## What counts

An **adopted repo** is a repo the app is installed on that has a dashboard: the action has run a
scan there. Only adopted repos count.

A repo without a dashboard never counts, so you can install the app on a whole org at no cost.
It is listed under Not set up yet, with its onboarding pull request, and counts from the moment
its first scan writes the dashboard.

Edit mode does not count either: it shows one repo at a time.

## Active repos, and the ones past the line

Your plan's number of adopted repos are **active**: the app shows them whole, on the org view,
their own pages, Insights and the audit log.

Which ones is your choice. On the org's **Settings** tab, under Active repos, pick up to your
plan's number. The places you leave open go to the busiest repos: the most stacks not in sync,
then the most stacks, then by name. With no choice, the busiest are active. Anyone who can see
the installation can choose, because the choice changes what the app shows and nothing else.
Clear the choice to go back to the busiest.

The adopted repos past the line are listed greyed out, in a group of their own called Past the
free tier, or past your plan's name:

- **The org view** lists each by name with its state counts, and no stacks or rows.
- **Their own pages** are not shown, so Rescan for them opens the dashboard on GitHub.
- **The audit log** says how many lines each one has, and not the lines. The CSV holds what the
  page shows.
- **Insights** name each one with its deploys, and do not count them.

<Callout kind="note" title="Nothing about deploying changes">
  A repo past the line keeps its dashboard on GitHub, and it works as always: scans, ticks and
  deploys go on exactly as they did. The app keeps its facts, and shows them again as soon as
  the repo is active.
</Callout>

## History

The audit log shows the deploys inside your plan's history, and its last sentence says how far
back that is and on which plan. Nothing is deleted at the line: when your plan grows, the older
lines are shown again.

Insights always cover the last 12 weeks against the 12 before, on every plan.

## Upgrade

An admin of the org on GitHub, or the person whose personal account it is, can change the plan.
Everyone else who can see the installation sees the plan, its numbers and its price, and one
sentence that says who can change it. The app checks with GitHub every time someone changes the
plan.

**Upgrade** is on the Settings tab, and in place of the pricing link beside the repos past the
line. Choose a plan, monthly or yearly, and pay on Stripe's Checkout page. There is no trial.

- **VAT**: Stripe works out the tax from your billing address. A business in the EU can enter
  its VAT id.
- **A promotion code** goes in on the Checkout page. A code that brings the price to 0 needs no
  card, and the Plan section names the code.

You come back to Settings, which says Stripe is confirming the payment until it has.

## Change or cancel

**Manage billing** on the Settings tab opens Stripe's customer portal. Change between Team and
Team Plus or monthly and yearly, update your card, read your invoices, or cancel there.

When you cancel, or a payment fails, your plan holds until the end of the period you paid for,
and the installation falls back to Free after it. Nothing is deleted: the app shows what Free
shows, and shows the rest again if a plan starts again. Settings says when your plan renews or
ends, and what then happens.

To pay by invoice, write to <a href="mailto:hello@sluiceway.dev">hello@sluiceway.dev</a>. An
invoice plan is changed the same way.
